What it is
----------
The speaker-embedding model used by Audio2Notes for speaker diarization. It turns a
short span of speech into a voiceprint; the app then clusters those voiceprints to
decide how many distinct voices are on the recording. It is the "who" half of
diarization — the bundled pyannote segmentation model (see
LICENSE-pyannote-segmentation-3-0.txt) is the "when" half, marking where speech
turns happen.

This file is a converted/quantized ONNX export of the CAM++ speaker-verification
model from the 3D-Speaker project, distributed by the sherpa-onnx project as a
release asset.

Why it is bundled
-----------------
Speaker recognition used to require a 27 MB download on first use, which made the
feature unavailable offline and added a network dependency to the very first
recording. Shipping the model removes that dependency. The per-user cache
(<userData>/models/speaker/) still takes precedence when present, so a user who
downloads a model explicitly runs what they downloaded; the bundled copy is the
fallback. See src/diarize.js: resolveEmbeddingModel().
